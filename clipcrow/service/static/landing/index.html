Two jobs ClipCrow does really well

Use case 1

Long talk, vox populi or podcast, cut into many reels

Upload one long recording: a speech, a panel, street interviews, a two-person podcast. ClipCrow picks the self-contained moments and gives you a set of vertical reels, each with a title, a hook and captions in the speaker's language.

  • 6 to 10 reels from one recording, chosen by what makes sense on its own.
  • Several people on screen? It cuts to whoever is talking, or stacks two.
  • A cover title, your logo and a watch-the-full-video banner on every reel.

For speakers, news and media channels, podcasters, event teams.

A 1-hour podcast becomes about 10 reels.

Use case 2

A raw take, turned into a clean, branded production

Record yourself talking to the camera, mistakes and all. ClipCrow removes the ums, stutters, restarted sentences, "sorry, once more" takes and long silences, keeping picture and sound in sync, then dresses it in your brand.

  • One tight video from your raw footage, ready to publish.
  • Your logo, a cover title, an ending video and music, set once and used every time.
  • Pick how tight: natural for a course, fast for YouTube, tight for Reels and Shorts.

For teachers, course creators, founders, coaches, YouTubers.

A 10-minute raw recording becomes one clean video.

From one recording to ready clips

  1. 1

    Drop your video

    Any long recording: a talk, a podcast, a class, an interview. Choose the language and how many clips you want.

  2. 2

    ClipCrow listens

    It writes word-by-word captions, then picks self-contained moments and writes a hook and a title for each.

  3. 3

    It frames and renders

    A vertical crop follows the speaker. Captions, your logo, a cover title and an ending video are added.

  4. 4

    You check and post

    Watch every clip, fix a word, change a cut, then download or post to your channels.

Questions

Which languages work?

English, Marathi, Hindi, Nepali and many more. Marathi, Hindi and Nepali get a recogniser that keeps the text in Devanagari.

Do I need to sign up with a password?

No. Enter your email, we send a 6-digit code, and you are in. The same step creates your account the first time.

What does it cost?

You pay for the minutes of video you upload, at one price per minute, from a balance you recharge with any amount. New accounts start with free minutes. Failed jobs give their minutes back automatically.

What happens to my videos?

They are used only to make your clips and are deleted automatically after the days your plan keeps them. You can delete them yourself at any time. Read the privacy policy.

Who owns the clips?

You do. You confirm you have the rights to the video you upload, and the clips are yours to publish.
